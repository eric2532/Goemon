Dragoon landing preview — latest Forest Shrine v2.59.0

While Dragoon is airborne or hovering, a translucent blue ground circle follows
the crosshair's landing solution. Its outer boundary is the full Dragon Leap
damage radius (19.8m before existing range modifiers), with faint rings for the
12.6m and 16.2m pulses. It shows all three expanding damage zones.

On launch, center and radius follow the committed target/rangeScale snapshot,
so moving the camera does not move the actual attack indicator. If collision
forces the strike into its straight-down fallback, the circle moves below the
real body to show the new landing area. No valid floor means no circle.
Landing, death, pause, cancel, reset and character changes hide the marker;
paused-tree end overlays also hide it via the marker's lightweight cleanup.

Rendering: one persistent PlaneMesh with a reusable transparent blue shader.
No per-frame mesh/effect creation; no shadows or light nodes. Aiming reuses the
same target/floor solver as the actual ability; damage and preview use the same
static radius function. Existing gameplay, animations, sweep and controls remain.
