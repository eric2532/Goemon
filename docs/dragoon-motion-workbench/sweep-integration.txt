Dragoon controls and Azure Lance Sweep — latest Forest Shrine v2.59.0

Grounded LMB: approved Lance Pierce thrust, unchanged.
Grounded RMB / 3: Azure Lance Sweep, replacing the old automatic leap.
Airborne LMB or RMB / 3: Dragon Leap toward the crosshair landing point.
Both aerial buttons ignore ground cooldowns and do not start a new timer.
Space: one jump at 3x ordinary height; hold to hover at apex / while descending.
Dragon Leap retains the 3x landing shockwave radii (12.6 / 16.2 / 19.8m base).

Sweep reuses the approved Blender thrust action. Its upper-body bones and lance
rotate together from left to right; approved lower-body pose and hand grips stay
coherent. No original Blender source, mesh, UVs or rest skeleton are changed.
The new sweep lasts .84s, with contacts at .22 / .34 / .46 authored combat seconds.
It deals three times normal thrust base damage per contact (37 x 3 = 111), with
three hits for 333 total base damage before the existing character/critical/combo
multipliers. One cast ticket owns all contacts, so the move earns one combo link.
Hits respect the forward semicircle, reach and solid cover; oversized boosted
steps still resolve three contacts, and canceled moves cannot release later hits.
The blue energy crescents match the wide frontal sweep. Grounded cooldown remains
2.6s, with the existing Dragon Leap buff still clearing Sweep/Back Flip cooldowns.

Sound Studio adds a separate Azure Lance Sweep Weapon/Voice pair. Existing
secondary recordings remain assigned to airborne Dragon Leap; its original voice
plays once on landing. Both aerial HUD icons show ready and say Dragon Leap.
