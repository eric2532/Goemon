Title: Spear, by Sounds of the Stickman. Five discrete effects.

Grounded left click uses the three source thrusts as combo variations, with a
stronger third hit. Grounded right click blends both cleaves with a quieter
thrust into one broad strike at first contact. It sounds once for the entire
three-hit swing; the asset contains no spaced damage-contact cues, so hit-stop
and attack-speed boosts do not put later audio beats ahead of gameplay.

The new files are scoped to Dragoon. Other characters keep their original
sounds, and airborne Dragon Leap keeps its separate slam. Move and shared
recordings, gains and mutes retain their existing priority. No user recordings
or settings are modified by the build. Sound Studio previews the new defaults.
