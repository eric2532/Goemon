Armored Dragon Leap — geometry revised 2026-10-25

The landing retains its three 64-base-damage shockwaves. A winged steel dragon
then makes piercing passes for three seconds, seeking visible enemies within
24 metres of its current position. It travels at 42 m/s, chooses a new dash
heading every 0.35 seconds, and deals 192 base damage once per victim per pass.
Existing damage, critical and range modifiers still apply. With no enemy, it
circles the landing point until expiry. Physical cover stops its current pass.
The player can move and attack while it hunts. Switching character, resetting
or cancelling the controller releases its cached projectile. Already released
native allied casts retain the same independent-cast lifecycle as other shots.

Editable dragon geometry: tools/build_dragoon_energy_dragon.gd.
Generated shared mesh: assets/rogue/effects/dragoon_energy_dragon.res.
Material and wing motion: assets/rogue/shaders/dragoon_energy.gdshader.
The user-approved concept is assets/references/dragoon_summon/approved-concept.png.
The revised black dragon has a forward-reaching wedge head, open jaw, red eyes,
paired swept horns, a plated neck/ribcage, tucked clawed limbs, curved scalloped
wings and a curled balancing tail. The existing tube/panel mesh bake is reused.
It contains 7,794 vertices and 5,744 triangles (before: 3,434 / 6,056).
One surface, one material per player lease or shared army batch, no textures,
no new skeleton or per-unit animation player. Vertex alpha tags wing vertices
for the shoulder-based shader flap; it does not control their transparency.
Normals rotate with the wings. Dissolve grain starts only during expiry, removing
the old early holes near the tail. Shader-expanded culling bounds contain both
wing extremes in the attached and hunting poses.

The procedural generator remains the authoritative editable source for this
particular effect. Geometry is authored in flight space (+Z muzzle-forward),
then inverse-transformed into the original +Y-up effect coordinates. Keep that
conversion and the shader flight basis together. The existing descent attachment,
projectile scale and native/player motion owners are retained. No character
Blender master, lance grip, leap body animation
or army pose atlas was changed. Rebuild the mesh through Godot with:
  --headless --path . --script res://tools/build_dragoon_energy_dragon.gd
