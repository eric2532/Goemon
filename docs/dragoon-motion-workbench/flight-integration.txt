Dragoon flight update — latest Forest Shrine v2.59.0

Space: one jump at approximately three ordinary jump heights, with default
launch speed sqrt(3) times baseline and the same gravity. Dragoon no longer has
a double jump; other characters retain their existing jump counts and bonuses.
Explicit user tuning overrides remain available through the existing balance UI.
Hold Space: reach full natural apex, then hold current altitude. Pressing/holding
Space while descending also suspends vertical motion. Release Space to resume
normal gravity. Hover does not add height. WASD retains normal air steering.

Airborne RMB / 3: freeze the crosshair floor target at activation and launch the
real collision body toward it at up to 45 m/s. Collision blocks walls. Real floor
contact emits the existing powerful three widening blue shockwaves (64 base damage
each), now at triple the original radii (12.6, 16.2, 19.8m before range upgrades). Reaching an empty-space goal or making no progress against a roof/wall
falls back to a straight downward dive so travel cannot trap controls. No damage
or landing effect is awarded merely for reaching an aim point.

Airborne RMB has no cooldown: it ignores any remaining grounded timer and does
not start or refresh one. Grounded RMB retains Azure Lance Leap and its normal
cooldown. The HUD shows aerial RMB ready while airborne. Repeated clicks during
an active dive cannot queue an unwanted grounded leap on landing.

For this pass, grounded RMB retains Azure Lance Leap. Left click remains lance
attacks on the ground and in the air. The user's optional suggestion to make all
airborne attacks dive and replace grounded RMB remains a future control choice.
The approved idle, lance attack, jump and jump-strike poses are retained.

Built from the live shared project. Verified latest other chat 'Improve 3D combat
feel' now includes sticky melee memory and paired Weapon/Voice Sound Studio
controls; these changes and current music/HUD/enemy counts are preserved.
