# Automatic construction · v2.45

Stand stationary beside an affordable empty tower or mine spot to start construction automatically. The nearest reachable footprint is selected; all sides remain usable and solid cover blocks work. B is no longer needed. Walking, jumping, rolling, knockback, leaving range and pauses stop work. Unattended unpaid progress decays; stopping nearby again resumes automatically. The existing tower duration/cost is three seconds/five gold; mines use four seconds/ten gold. Completion pays once.

Empty sites show four small muted stones instead of large foundations or purchasing platforms. The complete tower/mine silhouette appears nearby with a translucent jade material. A camera-facing circular progress ring starts at twelve o'clock and fills counterclockwise, with a percentage and price. Leaving hides the ghost and ring. Completion restores original imported materials and shadows, hides construction feedback and emits the supplied completion pulse. Mines and tower firing retain their original authored animations and gameplay after completion.

Automatic construction suspends automatic attacks and cancels held native attacks. Movement, jump, dodge, hero selection, pause and the J toggle remain available. Insufficient gold leaves combat available. The standard and custom maps share the interaction; custom map editor markers remain visible for authoring, while playable custom maps use the subtle construction presentation. Older Sky Descent/Tower Defense modes keep their own interaction.
