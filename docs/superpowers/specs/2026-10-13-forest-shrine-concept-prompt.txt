Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: Preview-only game design illustration for a discussion, not an implemented game screenshot or production asset.
Primary request: An original hand-drawn overhead map concept sheet for an Edo-inspired action and RTS survival game. One shrine to protect at the center of a large forest; the player freely builds mines, walls, gates and towers on a grid, assigns workers, and survives enemies approaching from unpredictable directions. Show the forest environment and how shared fog of war could look.

Composition: Wide landscape concept sheet with two large, clearly separated overhead map panels on warm parchment. Left panel titled exactly "TERRAIN CONCEPT". Right panel titled exactly "SHARED VISION". Small overall title "FOREST SHRINE". Very little other text. Both panels depict the same terrain geography with the same central shrine, forest clearings and winding passages, viewed directly from above for readability.

Terrain: A single small Japanese timber shrine with a red torii at the exact center of a broad grassy clearing. Dense varied cedar forest surrounds it on ALL sides, extending to the map edges. Irregular smaller clearings and many winding traversable gaps through the forest allow approaches from every compass direction. No fixed three lanes, no four symmetrical roads, no enclosing impassable tree ring, no river moat, no perimeter castle. A nearby exposed gold-bearing rock deposit offers a starter economy, and a few additional gold deposits lie farther out in woodland clearings. Ground has moss, roots, rock outcrops and readable paths. Enough buildable open ground for player-designed defenses.

Example player-built structures: In both panels include the same sparse, illustrative mid-run settlement: the one shrine, a small timber mine on the nearby deposit, one occupied wooden archery watchtower near a clearing edge, two short straight grid-aligned wooden palisade segments and one passable gate, a few tiny workers and a tiny hero. Leave most construction space empty. A faint square construction grid is visible only on a small patch of clearing. These are examples of player placements, not a finished fortress. No second shrine, castle keep, town or huge stone buildings.

Left map shows the terrain fully as an artist's design study. No enemy icons, no enemy spawn indicators, no red arrows. Right map illustrates a possible visibility state: warm fully readable terrain around the shrine, mine, tower, hero and workers; overlapping modest sight regions combine into shared vision. A narrow explored trail remains dim and desaturated after the hero has passed. Far unexplored forest is covered by dense dark painterly fog with soft irregular edges. The fog fully hides enemies, silhouettes and markers. No exposed enemy units anywhere. Tower sight extends farther than worker sight. The center must be readable and the fog visually unmistakable.

Style: Polished original hand-painted watercolor and gouache game concept art with fine ink contours, stylized Edo timber architecture, rich forest greens, warm earthen clearings and restrained gold deposits. Charming but strategically readable. Detailed canopy texture around clear ground; buildings and paths clearly legible. Minimal tasteful annotation, no elaborate UI, no command menu, no buttons, no decorative fantasy creatures, no watermark. This is a conceptual drawing, not a claim of exact grid dimensions or balanced sight radii.
