Original Musashi audio from the supplied USA retail disc.

Reproduce dry samples with tools/recover_musashi_audio.py. Optional --sound
arguments recover specific original dispatch IDs; audio_provenance.json retains
source addresses, banks, sample hashes, pitch/driver records and runtime aliases.

Fusion swings:0x400/0x446. Fourth-Fusion initializer voice:0x5F6, apex
follow-through:0x447, strong contact selector3:0x439. The third cut retains
0x438. Lumina initializer:0x455. Both swords share normal enemy contact0x437.
Rotation body event at tick11 triggers0x9DA. Identical dry PCM0x439/9DA shares
lumina_rotation.wav; confirmed0x5F6 shares the existing voice_exertion.wav.

Actual SC01 terrain0 footsteps use0x406/407 at original opcode8 markers.
Actual air landing61774->61D20 uses0x42E. These events share the identical
foot_contact.wav in the selected chapter bank. The older land.wav filename is
retained for0x41E ground-stop/skid (5AC48->61CD0); it is no longer used as the
ordinary landing default. Other terrain banks have not been mapped to the host
landscape. Source actor/material dispatch, rather than filename, identifies use.

Normal Fusion/Lumina call0x5F3, inactive in this US executable: their voice
defaults are silent. No confirmed jump voice is supplied. Damage/knockback uses
0x451. Existing custom recordings, mute and gain preferences remain available;
o synthesized grunt or substitute sound is introduced. Off-player voice policy
remains the host game's existing rule. Full story dialogue is not integrated.

Decoded original PCM and driver-derived playback rates are retained; integer
WAV rates are rounded. No replacement synthesis/denoising/normalization is used.
SPU ADSR, reverb, event volume and full hardware interpolation are not emulated.
