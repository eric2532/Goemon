map/entrance/return/gate ledger is generated by `tools/report_east_region.py`

`region-checklist.json`. The audit's former open branches have now been traced
through both continents, all four reachable fleets, and the special/ghost-ship
quests. Preparation is distinct from live route and visual verification.

twenty active-play seconds and automatic arrival at the original landing cell.
Pauses and dialogue hold that timer; original ship rooms, port coordinates,
clerk fares and continental quest admission remain with their existing owners.

skipping it. Generic harbor quest actors retain their original event programs.

## Initial audit, retained for provenance

The Il harbor is reached through an NPC in 伊爾村. A fixed warp search from village map `2000` therefore misses the entire harbor. The client cache establishes map identity and partial coverage. The fixed warp table and pinned community server scripts establish the connections described here. Current Explore Mode has no verified harbor crossings.
