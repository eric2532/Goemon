# Il and Alyut native prop derivatives

Run `python3 tools/prepare_east_village_props.py` and `--check` from the complete source. `data.json` records every original map object, the existing owner or selected visual, original cells/footprints/origins, native RGBA hashes and the resulting atlas rectangles. The source maps and native artwork remain intact. Only known checkerboard ground-shadow alpha is removed; every RGB pixel is preserved.

`scripts/forest/forest_east_village_props.gd` is a setup-time scenery helper. Il calls it from its existing native-object batch; Alyut calls it from its existing village scenery hook. Doors, collisions, navigation, events, NPCs and encounters retain their existing owners. Laruka and the West region do not use these replacements. Shared measured Tina forms are copied into the village height frame; original front UVs remain intact, while vertical UVs on tiled reverse faces retain their world texture pitch.

Reuse contracts:

- Graphics11044 and11082 use source-measured post feet, eaves and ridge anchors with existing coastal timber/roof primitives. Observed faces retain source paint; hidden sides use separate timber/thatch surfaces. Graphic11072 reuses coastal round stone/timber, native rock and roof primitives, with a source11072 stone crop retained in the derivative atlas. Graphic11014 uses the source2×4footprint, shared pitched roof/posts and stacked Tina log profiles with their original bark/end tiles. The stable is decorative and adds no room or route.
- Flowering trees12172–12174 fit individual source-measured crown sizes and native trunk feet using the existing Wind Meadow `cg_silverleaf` master. These volumes are an inferred 3D reconstruction. Pink flowers12175 and other fine flora retain native alpha artwork with shallow painted edges.
- Carts11022/11023 reuse the market-cart torus/spoke technique and coastal planks/bars, sized from the source wheels and footprints. Their decks tip toward the ground as in the original, with separate native wood tiles instead of projecting the whole front sprite around solid disks. Tied hay bundles and sheaves use closed source-shaped profiles. Stockades reuse the original measured source front and closed stone base; their reverse timbers use rounded Tina bark/end profiles and the observed4/3/3cut-log counts for11143/11145/11146, with the original outline and collider owner preserved. Round barrels, mushrooms, fences, headstones and furniture reuse the original-map/coastal geometry. Brick planters use the existing Il/Laruka brick atlas and Wind Meadow leafy shrub. The partly occluded barrel pile retains its complete native silhouette and paint as a shallow relief.
- Thin signs, tools, fences, clothes and irregular small cutouts retain source art instead of stretching its front face around a box. Edge depth, hidden well/stable structure, wood-board thickness, cart tilt, wheel/spoke depth, tied straw profiles, water depth and shared foliage geometry are inferred, without adding doors, rooms or paths.

Actual review evidence belongs in `build/Other Files/Asset Toolkit/East Gate Region/Village Props/` and `Verification/VillageProps/`. `east_village_props_review.gd` checks all126 source graphics and captures normal gameplay-camera views from two connected source walk-grid corners for the Il wells/garden and Alyut stable/both farm carts. A passing geometry check does not replace inspection of those images.
