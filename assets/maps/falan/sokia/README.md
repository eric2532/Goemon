work. This direction supersedes the narrower route/crop exclusions and bounded
“finished” or “completed review” labels in earlier Sokia, Venoa, Ruby and
South Gate–Kili reports. Those reports remain historical evidence, not proof
that this expanded goal is complete. **The regional goal remains unfinished.**

- Recover the entire outdoor area around both regions, including off-route
  ground, separate source components, coastline, cliffs, desert, side paths,
  dead ends, landmarks and cave mouths. Check original map layers/art tile by
  tile. Every source-confirmed entrance counts, including entrances absent from
  the current game and entrances controlled by NPC/event scripts. A road buffer,
  selected entry-component flood fill or existing runtime crop cannot define the
  whole required area. Record genuine region boundaries with source evidence;
  a previous report's exclusion is not evidence of one.
- Preserve original layout while translating the visible art into our stylized
  3D. Reconstruct unseen surfaces using the visible design and nearby examples;
  label that inference without changing footprints, routes or source stations.
  Such permitted unseen-surface fitting does not itself make a map missing or
  require identical 2D/3D pixels. Coverage still requires the source layout/art
  review and documented encounter rules.
- Reconcile each map, encounter zone and dungeon floor with original encounter
  tables and event scripts. Restore every confirmed enemy identity/appearance,
  rare variant, mixed group and boss. Preserve documented group membership,
  location, level ranges and relative weights. Keep safe zones safe and scripted
  fights behind their source triggers. Reuse the existing native army, encounter
  and shuffle-bag owners; a generic regional pool does not satisfy coverage.
- Verify the map/entrance and encounter inventories from source data, including
  maps or floors the current loaders omit. Use fixed normal gameplay-camera
  views for scenery and directly trigger representative fights, including rare
  groups and bosses, rather than waiting for random rolls. A data match, an
  overhead image or an old test alone does not verify rendered gameplay.
- Report **missing maps**, **missing enemy assets** and **unverified encounter
  rules** separately, with exact IDs, source evidence and the next required
  step. Keep blocked and unverified rows open. No-source evidence is not proof
  that a map is safe, and a preserved adapted boss is not proof of its original
  trigger, companions or frequency.
