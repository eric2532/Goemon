Source format interpretation:
func_80020A28 decodes 8-byte packed part transforms; bit24 of pose pointers
selects that packed format. Position is signed8 XYZ. Rotation is XYZ Euler in
12-bit turns. Source pose streams contain (pointer,signed16 duration,u16 flags)
pairs. func_8012AAAC handles their control records. These soldier streams use
flags0 and -1 loop termination. Attack/hurt/death completion is controlled by
actor AI state timers, not by changing the source stream's loop terminator.

Coordinate contract:
One source unit is represented as 0.01 meter. Blender C=(x,z,-y) converts source
to Blender Z-up. glTF export converts Blender to Y-up. Source forward -Z becomes
glTF/Godot +Z; rotate actor Y by PI for a controller facing Godot -Z.
Part local bounds in manifest.json use Blender Z-up meters; convert their
corners (x,z,-y) for glTF Y-up. Baked actor-space source matrices are recoverable
exactly from the sidecar angle/position records and this transform contract.

Timing and semantic mapping:
All animations retain discrete STEP keys. One source tick is presented at
nominal 30Hz. The semantic aliases do not rename or remove numeric actions.
Idle AFDD0 is used for sentry waiting after reload; walk AFF18 accompanies
source patrol velocity(0,0,-4); run AFFC0 accompanies pursuit velocity(0,0,-8).
The overlay confirms B0118/B01E0 melee, B0D50 firing, B0E48 reload, B02A8 hurt,
and B09D0 common death/tumble. Other reaction labels remain deliberately broad.
Gameplay contact/event timing is retained separately in gameplay_events, since
AI timers can differ from full animation duration. Do not assume every source
loop should run forever during gameplay or every retained clip is an attack.
