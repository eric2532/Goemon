Playable Musashi — recovered source derivative

This asset comes from the user's supplied Brave Fencer Musashi (USA) disc image.
The original recovery is preserved separately under:
build/Other Files/Musashi Asset Recovery/animation_recovery/

Part pointer list: 0x800DE2A4. Resident load base: 0x800CEDF8.

31 rigid body/weapon parts, 31 editable part bones plus Root, and 68 recovered
source motion actions. All 64 original actions and held poses are retained.
The part-bone hierarchy is a reconstructed editing rig because the source pose
format stores baked actor-space transforms rather than anatomical parent links.

The game displays this rig at 1.8x scale, with a half-turn to match its -Z forward
convention. Original animations are selected for idle, slow/run/sprint cycles,
standing/moving jumps, a double-jump flip, falling, landing, three Fusion cuts,
and grounded/airborne Lumina swings. Gameplay names are local mappings; physics,
damage, enemies, and effects belong to this game's controller.
This is a basic playable sword kit; original assimilation and scroll mechanics,
facial texture swaps, and all original PS1 gameplay are not implemented.

Requested reference palette: bright clothing reds, navy-indigo hair and brows
(#25275B), warm golden skin matching the original face atlas (#FFC67B), clean
whites, yellow gold and cool silver. tools/style_recovered_musashi.py creates
this derivative from the preserved GLB, encoding all untextured display RGB
in linear space. Geometry, texture pixels, rig weights and all 64 motion
sequences remain unchanged. Portrait is rendered from the styled game asset.

Motion update: tools/recover_musashi_motion_continuations.py appends four actions
without changing the first 64 or their binary animation data. It recovers the
standing jump continuation at 0x800D4AD4 (original stream 0x800D4A9C, loop tick 8),
moving jump continuation at 0x800D4B70 (stream 0x800D4B48, loop tick 7), and Fusion
cuts at 0x800D4CE4 / 0x800D4D38 that omit the model-list prefix. Original stream
base/PC are retained for opcode 6; inherited weapon flags and visibility events
16-19/25-26 are applied. motion_sources.json records addresses and pose hashes.
1,550 new joint/frame transformations round-trip against source matrices.

Runtime uses continuous stride phase, starts runs from their first pose, holds
the jump's pause pose during ascent, resumes its continuation on descent, and
plays landing only after floor contact. Each new airborne jump restarts the
source flip. Fusion contacts follow event 20 at ticks 2/3/6. Ground and air
Lumina use the recovered two-handed overhead release Extra__800d5098, with
swing audio at tick 13 and adapted contact at tick 15 of its 22-tick duration.
The visible trail samples each drawn blade: Fusion part22 and Lumina part25.
Air Fusion starts active at tick 0. Clip duration and
damage advance on the same clock, including attack-speed bonuses. Playback
uses the recovery's nominal 30 Hz timing; emulator-exact timing and a complete
recreation of the original game's combat mechanics are not claimed.
Source interpretation follows the pinned BFM-decomp revision:
https://github.com/Druthulu/BFM-decomp/tree/b6a28f96bac70af70573826f767a96cf212fd5e3
Relevant functions: 8015A3C8, 80161124, 8015AE2C, 8015B858, 801555F4, 8015444C.

Runtime materials use reference_palette.gdshader to compensate for the Forest
scene's Filmic tonemapper and exposure. This keeps the display-authored palette
consistent with the portrait while leaving the scenery's grading unchanged.
The inverse curve follows Godot 4.6's official tonemap_filmic implementation:
https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/4.6-stable/servers/rendering/renderer_rd/shaders/effects/tonemap.glsl
Rendered screenshot checks verify golden skin and navy hair within three RGB
levels of their authored display values. No original texture pixels are edited.

Reproduction:
python3 tools/style_recovered_musashi.py --source <recovered GLB> \
  --output assets/characters/recovered_musashi/musashi.glb
python3 tools/recover_musashi_motion_continuations.py --recovery-root <recovery directory> \
  --glb assets/characters/recovered_musashi/musashi.glb \
  --report assets/characters/recovered_musashi/motion_sources.json
Blender --background --python tools/render_recovered_musashi_portrait.py -- \
  assets/characters/recovered_musashi/musashi.glb <portrait output PNG>

Controls: WASD move; Shift sprint; Space jump/double jump; C evade;
LMB / hold LMB sword combo; RMB heavy sword strike; Q fighter selection.
Edo remains a separate fighter. Musashi's stable character ID is 13.
